# Whisper Model Benchmark

Test environment: dev server, 48 cores, 92GB RAM, CPU only, int8 quantization, beam_size=3.

Test audio generated by espeak-ng: ~9s English, ~12s Chinese, ~22s mixed.

## Results

| Model | Audio | Load | Transcribe | Lang | Quality |
|-------|-------|-----:|-----------:|------|---------|
| tiny | en | 0.4s | 0.6s | en | ❌ "rainy dog", "modern speed" (wrong words) |
| tiny | zh | 0.4s | 0.6s | zh | ❌ Garbage: 堅堅堅其恒恒... (unusable) |
| tiny | mixed | 0.4s | 0.9s | en | ❌ Lost Chinese entirely |
| base | en | 0.4s | 0.7s | en | ⚠️ "A quick round fox" (close but errors) |
| base | zh | 0.4s | 0.9s | **th** | ❌ Detected as Thai, complete garbage |
| base | mixed | 0.4s | 7.6s | **th** | ❌ Detected as Thai, unusable |
| **small** | **en** | **0.7s** | **1.8s** | **en** | **✅ Perfect transcription** |
| **small** | **zh** | **0.7s** | **1.8s** | **zh** | **✅ Correct (traditional chars, minor word diff)** |
| **small** | **mixed** | **0.7s** | **6.7s** | **en** | **⚠️ English perfect, Chinese degraded** |
| medium | en | 1.8s | 4.7s | en | ✅ Perfect transcription |
| medium | zh | 1.8s | 5.0s | zh | ✅ Perfect transcription |
| medium | mixed | 1.8s | 4.7s | en | ⚠️ Lost Chinese part entirely |

## Analysis

### tiny / base — Not viable
- Chinese recognition is completely broken (garbage output or wrong language detection)
- English has noticeable word errors
- base misidentifies Chinese as Thai

### small — Best trade-off ⭐
- **English**: perfect accuracy, 1.8s for 9s audio (5x realtime)
- **Chinese**: correct content, outputs traditional characters (acceptable)
- **Load time**: 0.7s (fast cold start)
- **Transcribe**: 1.8s per ~10s clip (responsive enough for interactive use)

### medium — Diminishing returns
- Slightly better Chinese (simplified chars, perfect punctuation)
- **2.5-3x slower** than small for marginal quality gain
- 1.8s load time (matters if worker restarts)
- Mixed audio: also loses Chinese, no advantage over small

### Mixed audio — Universal weakness
- All models struggle with mid-sentence language switching
- This is a known whisper limitation; not model-size dependent
- Workaround: set `--language` explicitly if you know the language

## Recommendation

**Use `small` for interactive voice input.** The 1.8s transcription time keeps the
feedback loop tight. Switch to `medium` only if you need perfect Chinese punctuation
and can tolerate 3x slower speed (e.g., batch subtitle generation via `main.py`).
